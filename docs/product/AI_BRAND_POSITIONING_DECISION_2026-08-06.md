**Status:** APPROVED BY PRODUCT OWNER
**Decision date:** 2026-08-06
**Scope:** Mercasto web, public metadata, acquisition pages and shared product copy

## Approved positioning

The product owner explicitly approved this Russian canonical message:

> Самая современная и умная доска объявлений с AI

Approved primary equivalents:

- Spanish: `La plataforma de clasificados más moderna e inteligente con AI`
- English: `The most modern and intelligent AI-powered classifieds platform`
- Russian: `Самая современная и умная доска объявлений с AI`

Equivalent localized wording is approved for every supported language.

## Decision precedence

This decision supersedes the shorter canonical wording documented in PR #496 and the revert rationale in PR #498. PR #498 explicitly allowed a change when the product owner approved a different canonical positioning; that approval is now recorded here.

The approved message must remain consistent across the shared application shell, authentication, publishing, seller acquisition, account screens, SEO metadata, official About/FAQ copy and standalone public policy pages.

## Change control

Do not automatically remove `intelligent`, `умная`, `inteligente`, or their approved localized equivalents. A future wording change requires another explicit product-owner decision and an update to this document and the AI brand contract test.
